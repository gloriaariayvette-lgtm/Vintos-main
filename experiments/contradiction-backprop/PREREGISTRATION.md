# Contradiction back-propagation: pre-registered evaluation

Written 2026-11-14, before any benchmark network was generated or scored. The module under test is
`scripts/belief_revision.py`; its constants (priors, thresholds 0.80 / 0.50 / 0.30, EXACT_LIMIT 14,
SAMPLES 20000) are frozen as committed with this file. The generator is `bench.py` in this directory.

## Data

Synthetic belief networks only (the repository is public; nothing of his is used here).
Four scenarios, generated by `bench.py`:

- **single**: one planted faulty source, 6-12 sources, one or two inference layers.
- **multi**: two or three planted faulty sources.
- **noisy**: one or two planted faults, plus at least one of: a wrong witness (a planted-faulty
  correction or observation contradicting something true), a false-alarm contradiction between two true
  beliefs (reported reliability 0.9), noisy confirmations (accuracy 0.85, including a false node wrongly
  confirmed).
- **chain**: inference chains three to five steps deep; a planted fault may be an inference step rather
  than a source.

Each source carries kind, stated confidence, reinforcement count and age. These are informative but
noisy: faulty sources tend to have fewer reinforcements and lower stated confidence; age is independent
of truth. Which source is faulty is drawn with a kind-dependent rate that is the module's prior multiplied
by a random factor between 0.5 and 1.5, so the generator does not share the module's priors exactly.

- **Development seeds** 0-99 per scenario: used only to check that the code runs. No constant was tuned on them.
- **Test seeds** 1000-1149 per scenario (600 networks): scored **once**.

## Methods

- **TMS-Bayes** (`belief_revision.plan`): revised set = sources with action retract or lower, plus her
  words flagged for her with blame >= 0.50 (the module would have lowered them, but leaves her word to her).
  Its top-1 is the highest-blame candidate; its withdrawn beliefs are those `plan` marks "withdraw".
- **Most recent**: for each contradiction, revise the most recent assumption among its candidates.
- **Lowest confidence**: for each contradiction, revise the candidate with the lowest stated confidence.
- **Uniform blame**: blame is spread evenly; every candidate is lowered. Its top-1 is the expected
  value of a uniform pick.

Every method is given the same candidates: the union of the contradiction's minimal conflict sets.
That is generous to the baselines, which today would not have provenance at all.

## Metrics

1. **Blame top-1**: over contradictions whose candidates hold at least one planted fault, the fraction
   where the method's single most-blamed candidate is a planted fault.
2. **Precision and recall of the revised set** against detectable faults (planted faults that are a
   candidate of at least one contradiction), pooled over networks.
3. **Collateral damage**: (a) true candidates that were revised, as a share of all true candidates;
   (b) true derived beliefs withdrawn, as a share of true derived beliefs resting on any candidate.
   For the baselines, (b) counts a derived belief as withdrawn when every environment holds a revised source.
4. **Convergence**: the share of networks where every contradiction holding a planted fault is resolved
   (each of its conflict sets holds a revised source) and a second pass, with the revised sources
   treated as known wrong, revises nothing new. For the baselines the second-pass condition is the
   resolution itself, since they would pick again on whatever is unresolved.

## Bars (TMS-Bayes, all test networks pooled unless stated)

| Metric | Bar |
|---|---|
| Blame top-1 | >= 0.75 overall; >= 0.60 on noisy; at least 0.10 above every baseline overall |
| Precision | >= 0.75 |
| Recall | >= 0.70 |
| Collateral (a) | <= 0.10 |
| Collateral (b) | <= 0.10 |
| Convergence | >= 0.90 |

## Development log (amendments made on the development seeds, before the test split was ever generated)

The bars above were written first and were not changed. The development seeds turned up three design
problems in the module, fixed before freezing; no prior or threshold constant was changed.

1. A single contradiction with several equally plausible suspects rarely pushes any one of them past 0.50,
   so most contradictions were left unresolved (dev convergence 0.61, recall 0.55). Added: the most probable
   explanation of all contradictions together (exact over each component, or the best seeded draw refined
   greedily) names which sources to lower, in proportion to their blame. Her words in that explanation are
   flagged for her, and count as revised in the metrics.
2. Downstream re-derivation used an absolute 0.5 standing, which withdrew true beliefs that merely rest on
   several inference steps (dev collateral (b) 0.22-0.32). Now only beliefs built on a revised source are
   re-derived; one is withdrawn when every justification holds a revised source AND its standing fell by
   more than half; otherwise lowered.
3. A downstream secondary figure was added (not barred): the share of false beliefs resting on a candidate
   that were withdrawn or lowered.

Last development run (seeds 0-99, all scenarios), for the record: TMS-Bayes top-1 0.73, precision 0.66,
recall 0.67, collateral (a) 0.09, (b) 0.04, convergence 0.99; most recent 0.09; lowest confidence 0.59;
uniform 0.33. On development it misses the top-1, precision and recall bars. The module and generator were
frozen at this point and committed before the test split was run.

Passing every bar is what "real accuracy" means here. A missed bar is reported as missed; nothing
is re-run with different constants after the test score is seen.
