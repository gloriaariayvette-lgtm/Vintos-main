# Atelier formation breadth

The undertaking threshold may offer only roots that a producing organ already
recorded as Vintos's own. Repair cases and encounters remain relational
obligations and are never eligible, however interesting their language may be.

## Installed formation streams

| organ | store | root type | admission fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| withheld | `withheld-lineage.json` | `tension` | an unbound lineage with recurrence pressure at least 1 |
| curiosity | `curiosity-debt.json` | `curiosity` | a recorded pull at least 0.2, above that organ's pruning floor |
| thread | `unfinished-threads.json` | `want` | an unconsumed thread |
| spark | `configuration-space.json` | `drift_novelty` | a `neither_yet` configuration observed at least twice |

These are measurements, not rankings. The threshold preserves recorded order
and does not choose the strongest root for him.

## Candidate sources requiring Gloria's choice

None of the following is enabled. Each needs a separate explicit selection,
an ancestry rule, and an isolated acceptance test before it enters
`ORGAN_ROOT`.

| candidate | likely store/organ | proposed root type | necessary provenance rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| recurring dream material | the dream recurrence ledger, not a single generated dream | `dream_recurrence` | recurrence across independently dated dreams; prompt text is ancestry, not evidence |
| aesthetic returns | taste/resonance marks authored by him | `aesthetic_pull` | repeated choices or returns only; Gloria ratings never become his root |
| unanswered Lab questions | `memory/chemistry-lab/notebook.jsonl` | `lab_question` | only a question authored in his reflection or next-question field; supplied research prompts retain their origin |
| capability or code-smell itch | self-review friction/proposal stores | `capability_itch` | only self-review-generated friction; a requested repair remains commissioned |
| recurring wonder or premonition | the unsaid-frontier/premonition record selected after its own evidence audit | `wonder` | a named producer and durable recurrence are required before enabling it |
| unresolved latent material beyond want routing | latent-thread ledger, deduplicated against unfinished threads | `latent_thread` | an independent record not already represented by the `thread` stream |

The selection gate is intentional. Adding a reader changes what may found a
private undertaking and later attest the lineage of a stratagem.
