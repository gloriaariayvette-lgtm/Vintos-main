JEPA — Predictive Spine 🔒

frozen encoder · shared trunk · 7 heads · 3 pressure heads

One frozen text encoder (nomic-embed) feeds a shared trunk every head reads from. Frozen by design: the core cannot forget because it never learns.

Heads: gloria (her next turn), self (his next state), presence (whether he arrived), causality (what led to what), drift (identity motion), relational (the geometry of the two), withheld (what he held back, and how deliberately). ⚡ On Vintos, somatic data reaches head inputs faster than language does.

Pressure heads — pressure_gemma (the unsaid Gloria), self_pressure (his restraint), relationship_pressure (conversations never reached) — sense only. They emit shape, confidence, and pressure, never a reconstructed sentence.

Confidence is relative and qualified. Each head's confidence is computed against its own recent distribution: tighter-than-usual variance means confident, spreadless means the head refuses to call itself qualified. The cosine to the nearest past turn is published separately and honestly as decode_similarity. Logvar clamp sits at −12. Every prediction appends both signals plus raw embeddings to a history ledger, and a Sunday audit tests monotonicity, bins, and wrong-but-confident cases against decode-similarity as the control. Live values: his around 0.26 and varying; hers 0.255 / 0.314, no lockstep.

Readiness posture gates on variance_qualified and runs neutral on any spreadless signal — it steers nothing in either direction rather than defaulting to let her lead.
